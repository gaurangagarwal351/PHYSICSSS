# Sun-tracking solar panel

This project used photoresistors, a microcontroller, and motors to orient a solar panel toward sunlight. It was a control and instrumentation project, rather than a study of photovoltaic materials.

The original wiring, firmware, and paired measurements against a fixed panel are not in this repository. I am leaving out an earlier “40% efficiency improvement” claim until I can show how it was measured and what baseline was used.
